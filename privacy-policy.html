CloQ — Smart Alarm Clock

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

CloQ is developed and operated by Sartaz Amin, based in the United Kingdom. This privacy policy explains what information CloQ collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

We believe privacy should be simple. CloQ is designed to work almost entirely on your device — your data is yours.

Information We Collect

CloQ collects the minimum information necessary to provide its services.

Stored on your device only — never transmitted:

  • Alarm settings including times, repeat days, and chosen sounds
  • Medication reminder labels — short personal labels only such as Med A or Morning Pill. Actual medication names are never asked for or stored.
  • Dose tracking logs and streak counts
  • Voice recordings you create within the app
  • Voice messages received from friends via the wake-up nudge feature
  • App preferences and settings

Anonymous leaderboard data — only if you opt in:

  • A randomly generated anonymous identifier created on first launch
  • Your chosen age group
  • Your total daily screen time in minutes
  • No name, email, location, or any personally identifiable information is ever collected for the leaderboard

Information We Do Not Collect

CloQ never collects:

  • Your real name
  • Your email address
  • Your location
  • Actual medication names or medical information
  • Health records or clinical data
  • Payment or billing information — this is handled entirely by Apple
  • Any data without your explicit knowledge and action

How We Use Your Information

All alarm and reminder data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any server. Voice recordings are stored locally on your device only. Friend voice messages are stored locally on your device only.

The only data that ever leaves your device is anonymous leaderboard data, and only when you explicitly opt in through the Settings screen. You can opt out and delete this data at any time.

Third Party Services

Google AdMob — used to display advertisements to free users. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalisation in accordance with Google's policies. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings under Privacy. View AdMob Privacy Policy

RevenueCat — used to manage premium subscription purchases and verify transactions. RevenueCat processes purchase data on our behalf. View RevenueCat Privacy Policy

All payments are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. CloQ never sees, stores, or has access to your payment information.

Permissions We Request

  • Notifications — required to fire alarms and medication reminders at your scheduled times. Without this the core alarm functionality cannot work.
  • Microphone — requested only when you choose to record a custom alarm sound. You are explicitly asked for permission before any recording begins. We never record audio passively or without your direct action.
  • Contacts — requested only if you use the Friends feature to invite people from your contacts. Contact data is never uploaded to our servers and is only used to compose the invitation on your device.

Medical Disclaimer

CloQ is a personal reminder tool only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment guidance. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's guidance regarding your medication. Never adjust your medication based on reminders from this app.

Children

CloQ is rated 4+ on the App Store and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through CloQ please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

Data Retention

All data stored on your device remains there until you delete the app or clear its data through your device settings. Anonymous leaderboard data can be removed at any time by turning off the leaderboard toggle in Settings — this immediately removes your entry from the leaderboard.

Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a UK resident you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — you can request a copy of any data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — you can request correction of any inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — you can request deletion of your data
  • Right to portability — you can request your data in a portable format
  • Right to object — you can object to processing of your data

Since CloQ stores almost all data locally on your device, you can exercise most of these rights simply by deleting the app. For any data held by third party services (AdMob, RevenueCat) please refer to their respective privacy policies linked above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time as the app evolves. We will notify you of any significant changes through an in-app notice. The date at the top of this page always shows when it was last updated. Continued use of CloQ after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
